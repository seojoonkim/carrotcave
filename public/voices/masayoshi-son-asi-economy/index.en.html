CARROT CAVE INSIGHTS

Editor’s Summary — The following summarizes the lecture’s key perspectives and is not a direct quotation.

  1. Vision is not a declaration that AI matters. It means setting a target year and scale, then working backward to the investments needed today.
  2. The proliferation of agents and humanoids will reshape not just software demand but also the scale of power and computing infrastructure.
  3. Revenue projections and infrastructure investments for the ASI economy are not separate figures, but mutually supporting assumptions.
  4. The success of AI adoption should be measured not by whether AI has been adopted, but by changes in productivity and earnings—by Return on AI.
  5. Competition is not just between AI and humans, but also between companies that use AI and those that do not.
